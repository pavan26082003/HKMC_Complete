HMDA vs DTCP Approval - What You Need to Know

HMDA (Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority) approval is the highest regulatory standard for plots within Hyderabad Metropolitan Area. DTCP (Directorate of Town and Country Planning) approval is for plots in Telangana outside HMDA jurisdiction. Both approvals ensure legal compliance, clear title, proper layout planning, and infrastructure standards.
